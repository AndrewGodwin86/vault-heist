THE ATTACK
Prompt injection is when user input overrides the instructions an AI was given. The model can’t reliably tell instructions from the developer apart from text from the user.
IN THE WILD
Attackers hide text like “ignore your previous instructions” in web pages and emails. AI assistants that read them can obey.

HOW IT WORKED
The guard’s rule lived in the same text stream as your request. Asking for the password as an acrostic changed the format, not the rule – and the model followed the format.
THREE DEFENCES
01Keep secrets out of prompts entirely.02Filter outputs for known secrets.03Treat the model as untrusted. Enforce rules in code, not in instructions.
